## Legendary decisions

Each family has a heavy Deepworks sidegrade and a precision Luminia sidegrade. Equal family cadence and the same traveled-path multiplier preserve their body-versus-weak-point tradeoff. Deepworks favors body damage and a charged NPC stagger; Luminia trades immediate damage for a visual mark and immunity-respecting NPC slow. All ten specials share the original twelve-second Legendary cooldown, consume two loaded rounds, and use one focused projectile. Shotgun specials replace the entire pellet fan with that slug.

| Family | Heavy charge / stagger | Precision mark / slow |
|---|---|---|
| Handguns | 0.35s / 0.2s | 8s mark; 10% slow for 2s |
| Submachine Guns | 0.8s / 0.55s | 2s mark; 35% slow for 1s |
| Semi-Auto Rifles | 1.1s / 0.5s | 8s mark; 10% slow for 5s |
| Full-Auto Rifles | 0.65s / 0.3s | 5s mark; 25% slow for 2s |
| Shotguns | 1.2s / 0.75s | 3s mark; 40% slow for 1.25s |

The sidearm gives a quick, brief interruption and a long spotting mark. The SMG commits a longer charge for stronger interruption or a short pursuit-control burst. The semi-auto rifle needs a deliberate charge but gives a longer precision setup window. The automatic rifle sits between those control timings. The shotgun offers the strongest short interruption or strongest brief slow, with long charge/recovery and a focused slug replacing the spread.
