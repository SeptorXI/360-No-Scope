## External game and mod dependencies

Hytale, its server API and assets, and required third-party mods remain the property of their respective owners. Dependency JARs, native game archives and complete third-party vendor inventories are not distributed in this repository. Supply the dependencies separately as described in [installation](README.md).

The display equipment actor preserves native Mannequin attachment bone names and pivots for interoperability. Its visible native meshes are removed and its replacement texture is transparent. Furniture meshes and pixel atlases are authored project assets.
