360 NO SCOPE 2.4.7 — RECORDED FIRING AUDIO

Free Firearm Sound Library recordings by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney.
CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Original project — https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/bjjaszcz/the-firearm-sound-library/faqs
Source mirror — https://github.com/buddingmonkey/FreeFirearmsSFXLibrary/tree/beb2f4041f3d6740fa0aeaf0e71159bd65a78c1b

Each gun has its own set of three primary reports and recorded outdoor returns. The primary reports use edited single-microphone recordings. The returns use decay windows from distance recordings of the same recorded models.
The Goliath .50 uses the Springfield 1917 recording and Rampart SAW uses the AR-15 recording as sound analogs.
