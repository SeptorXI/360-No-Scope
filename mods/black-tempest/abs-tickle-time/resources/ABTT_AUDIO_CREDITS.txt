AB's Tickle Time 0.4.4 - Audio references and credits

Opening thunder: Dynamic Seasons 7.1.4 by BlueOrbit.
Existing Common/Sounds/Environments/Global/Weather/Lightning.ogg is
referenced by the gauntlet's immediate start event. Requires the installed
BlueOrbit:DynamicSeasons 7.1.4 asset pack. No Dynamic Seasons audio bytes
are bundled with this plugin.

Fingertip electricity: edited from "Elemental Spell Impact (Electric)" by
DRAGON-STUDIO via Pixabay. The six short edits are integrated into the
gauntlets' positional attack under the Pixabay Content License.
https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/technology-elemental-spell-impact-electric-448567/
https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/

Q storm wave crackles: existing Hytale Goblin Electric Sparks A01 through
A11. Hytale and its supplied assets retain their own terms. No Hytale sound
bytes are bundled with this plugin.

Bundled release discharge: edited powerdown.wav from Electricity Game
Sound Pack by faxcorp, released under CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://opengameart.org/content/electricity-game-sound-pack
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
It retains the existing short mono48k edited release from version 0.1.0.

The earlier BigSoundBank/Tesla/snap-loop onset and channel selections are
retired from this release. Their source credits and artifacts are archived
under authoring audio/revisions/0.1.0, rather than selected for playback.
The private authoring/audition WAVs are not plugin redistribution assets.
